# I Am Image Salon — website + booking portal

Premium rebuild of **I Am Image Salon** (1500 NW 18th Ave, Suite 104, Portland, OR 97209 · (503) 224-0399), replacing her Google Sites + Square Online pages. Built by Couture House Co.

- **Website:** home, full menu (74 real services & prices), 7 service landing pages, stylists, gallery, about, visit/contact, careers, journal.
- **Booking portal (demo):** 5-step booker with live price/time/deposit total, client account with the **Image Journey** photo timeline + Image Club rewards, owner studio (every stylist’s calendar, clients, finances, payroll, menu & hours editor) and stylist studio (own book only).
- **Signature:** a red line-art woman whose hair redraws from locs → braids → silk press → pixie; the logo’s two faces draw themselves on scroll; “Spin the Spiral” gift popup.

See **START-HERE.md** to run and deploy, **ASSETS-OWED.md** for what the salon still needs to send, **QA-REPORT.md** for the audit, **DESIGN.md** for the system.
