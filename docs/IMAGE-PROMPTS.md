# Image prompts (Kie.ai) — reproducible

All line art was generated on Frederick’s Kie.ai account with **Nano Banana Pro** (`nano-banana-pro`, 16:9, 2K, PNG). The locs frame is the master; every other woman frame is an **edit of that exact image** (passed as `image_input`) so her face, pose and framing never change — only the hair. Frames were then traced to SVG centerlines (scikit-image skeletonize → chain walk → RDP → Catmull-Rom curves), split into shared “base” lines (face/shoulders) and per-style hair lines, and merged into ~10 bucket paths per layer.

Real photos were upscaled 2x with **Recraft crisp upscale** (`recraft/crisp-upscale`) — no generative changes.

Source PNGs: `approval-stills/` next to this repo.
